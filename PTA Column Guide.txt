PTA DISPATCH WORKBOOK GUIDE
===========================

Recognized filenames
--------------------
Fleet_PTA_Finder.xlsx / XLSM
PTA_Dispatch_Tracker.xlsx / XLSM
PTA_Dispatch_Tracker_Updated_FIXED.xlsx

PTA Dispatch Tracker sheet
--------------------------
Preferred sheet name: PTA Tracker

Recognized columns:
Truck # / Truck / Unit
Div # / Division
Driver
PTA
Status
Plans / Preplan / Plan Status
Plan
Team
Destination / Area
OM
Count
Notes

The webpage recalculates hours past or until PTA from the PTA date/time.

Fleet PTA Finder sheets
-----------------------
Preferred sheet names:
Settings
Available
Dispatched

Recognized list columns:
Truck
Division
Driver
PTA
Status
Preplan
Flag
Type
Area
Miles / OM
Count

Settings may include Soon window (hours). The dashboard displays that window
but trusts the Available and Dispatched sheets as the filtered queues.

Plain-English alerts
--------------------
Critical: 24+ hours past PTA
High: 8-24 hours past PTA
Medium: 2-8 hours past PTA
New overdue: 0-2 hours past PTA
Due soon: finder row that is not yet past PTA

These are work-priority flags, not blame findings. Confirm driver readiness,
load availability, hours, equipment, and dispatch conditions.
