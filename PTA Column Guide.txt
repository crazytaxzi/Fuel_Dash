PTA DISPATCH DATA GUIDE
=======================

PTA reports are recognized from worksheet structure and column content. Filenames and worksheet names are not required.

COMMON COLUMNS
--------------
Truck # / Truck / Unit
Div # / Division
Driver
PTA
Status
Plans / Preplan / Plan Status
Plan
Team
Destination / Area
OM / Miles
Count
Notes
Flag
Type

The dashboard identifies tracker-style tables from truck, driver, PTA, status, and planning fields. Finder-style queues are identified from PTA, preplan, availability or dispatch status, and action flags.

The webpage recalculates hours past or until PTA from the PTA date and time. When several compatible tables exist, the strongest structural match and newest source are preferred.

PLAIN-ENGLISH ALERTS
--------------------
Critical: 24+ hours past PTA
High: 8-24 hours past PTA
Medium: 2-8 hours past PTA
New overdue: 0-2 hours past PTA
Due soon: recognized row that is not yet past PTA

These are work-priority flags, not blame findings. Confirm driver readiness, load availability, hours, equipment, and dispatch conditions.
