Connect your fuel-report folder

For the idle dashboard, choose the data folder containing summary, Detail, driver metrics detail, Driver Details, and rolling 7 day. The legacy workbook set and the older four basic reports are also supported: Driver Fuel Metrics, Fuel Compliance Analysis, Fuel Noncompliant Cost Analysis, and MPG by Driver. Optional electric APU and PTA dispatch workbooks can be included too. The dashboard recognizes Fleet PTA Finder and PTA Dispatch Tracker files automatically. XLSX and XLSM are accepted; the older basic set also accepts matching text-based PDFs.
