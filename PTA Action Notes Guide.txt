PTA ACTION NOTES
================

1. Open the PTA Dispatch page.
2. Click OPEN for a truck.
3. Type why you held, dispatched, called, reassigned, or otherwise handled it.
4. Click Save action note, or press Ctrl+Enter.

The dashboard keeps a timestamped history for each truck. It also captures the
PTA, status, plan status, and destination that were showing when the note was
saved. Notes live in this browser's local storage. They are not written into the
Excel files and do not sync to other computers.
