XLSX AND PDF REPORT GUIDE
=========================

The dashboard accepts supported XLSX and text-based PDF reports. Each file is classified from its contents, not its filename.

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS
--------------------

Recognizable reports normally expose enough of the following structure to establish their role:

- Driver identity, driver code, dispatch MPG, idle percentage, or out-of-route percentage
- Compliance dates and percentages
- Gallon, location, and total noncompliant cost fields
- Driver MPG history
- Transaction dates, units, gallons, locations, and purchase details

Files may be mixed between XLSX and PDF. When two files confidently represent the same role, the strongest structural match is used, with newer data breaking close ties.

PDF REQUIREMENTS
----------------

- PDFs must contain selectable text.
- Image-only scans need OCR first.
- Report titles, table headers, dates, percentages, and totals should remain visible.
- A redesigned layout may require another structural rule.

AVAILABLE ANALYSIS
------------------

The dashboard populates only the metrics supported by the recognized data. These can include weekly compliance, driver MPG, idle, out-of-route percentage, noncompliant cost, MPG history, driver notes, transaction drilldowns, and shift-transition export.

Missing fields remain blank. The dashboard does not convert an absent source into a suspiciously tidy zero.
